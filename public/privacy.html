Clearflow RO

Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Clearflow RO helps water purifier businesses manage their customers, service jobs, products, quotations, and invoices. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, and password when you sign up or accept an invitation. Business owners, technicians, and shops may also add a business name, business address, and GSTIN.
  • Business records: information a business enters about its customers, such as name, phone number, email, address, GSTIN, state, installed purifier, and notes, plus service jobs, products, prices, quotations, and invoices.
  • Feedback: issues, feedback, and feature requests you submit, with your name and contact details.
  • Activity log: which account added, changed, or deleted a business record, and when, so business owners can review changes.
  • Error reports: when the app hits an unexpected error, it sends the error message, the screen it happened on, your browser or device type, and the app version, linked to your account if you are signed in. We use these only to fix problems.

To let you view your data without a network connection, the app keeps a copy of your business's records on your device; it is removed when you sign out. The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers, and it does not show ads or use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To let your business and its team manage customers, service jobs, and invoices.
  • To send account emails, such as confirmations, invitations, and password resets.

We do not sell your information or share it with anyone for advertising.

Who can see your information

Each business's data is kept separate. Only signed-in members of the same business can see its records, limited by their role. Customers see only their own details, service history, and invoices.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores the app's data and handles sign-in.
  • Brevo delivers the app's account emails.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the web version of the app.

These providers process information only to run the service. All data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (More, then My account) or on the account deletion page. If you are a customer, technician, or shop, the business that invited you may keep its own service and invoice records about you for service and tax purposes; contact that business to have them removed.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or complete it, ask us to erase it, nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf, and raise a grievance. Business owners can correct customer and team details directly in the app. For anything else, contact the grievance officer below.

Children

Clearflow RO is a business app for adults and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
