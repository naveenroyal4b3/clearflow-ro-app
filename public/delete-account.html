Clearflow RO

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Clearflow RO account and its data at any time, from the app or from this website.

Delete from the website

Sign in and delete my account

Sign in with your Clearflow RO email and password, type DELETE to confirm, and your account is deleted immediately.

Delete in the Android app

  1. Open Clearflow RO and sign in.
  2. Tap More, then My account. On a large screen, select your name at the bottom of the side menu.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete account.

What is deleted

  • Technician, shop, and customer accounts: your login (name, email, phone, and business details on your profile) and the feedback you submitted are deleted immediately.
  • Business owners: if you are the only owner, the whole business workspace is deleted immediately, including all customers, service jobs, products, invoices, and feedback, and the logins of everyone you invited.

What may be kept

If you are a customer, technician, or shop, the business that invited you keeps its own records about you, such as your customer details, service history, and invoices, because it needs them for service and tax purposes. To have those removed, contact that business directly.
